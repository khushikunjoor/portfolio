User Research + Analytics

Campus Micromobility

Analyzing student discussions to identify transportation and safety concerns and turn unstructured feedback into insights for university decision-makers.

01 Discovery
02 Research
03 Analysis
04 Recommendations
01

Discovery

Problem

The university needed to better understand student experiences and concerns surrounding campus micromobility.

Evidence

Student discussions on Reddit provided unstructured, naturally occurring feedback.

Decision

Use student-generated discussions as a source of qualitative and quantitative product insight.

Outcome

A research approach centered around actual student experiences rather than assumptions.

02

Research

Evidence

More than 500 Reddit posts and comments were collected for analysis.

Approach

Built a SQL Server database and developed Python pipelines using the Gemini API to collect, clean, and organize data.

Decision

Narrow the dataset to discussions most relevant to transportation and safety.

Outcome

Unstructured conversations became a usable research dataset for analysis.

03

Analysis

Question

What recurring problems were students experiencing?

Evidence

Engagement and sentiment analysis were used to identify recurring themes and concerns.

Tools

Python, SQL Server, Gemini API, and data visualization.

Outcome

Transportation and safety patterns could be communicated through visual evidence rather than isolated comments.

04

Recommendations

Problem

Raw analysis needed to be translated into information leadership could act on.

Decision

Present the most recurring transportation and safety concerns alongside supporting visualizations.

Communication

Presented findings and recommendations to the Health, Well-Being, and Safety Department.

Outcome

Findings informed future discussions around campus transportation and student safety.

Product takeaway

This project taught me how to move from raw user feedback to structured research, measurable patterns, and recommendations that stakeholders can use.
