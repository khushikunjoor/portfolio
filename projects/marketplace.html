Product Analytics

Marketplace Analytics

Using product and operational data to give Product teams visibility into vehicle inventory changes and support marketplace decision-making.

01 Discovery
02 Data Analysis
03 Validation
04 Decision Support
01

Discovery

Problem

Product teams needed better visibility into how vehicle inventory changed throughout its lifecycle.

Evidence

Inventory activity included newly added, updated, sold, and deleted vehicles across large datasets.

Decision

Build reporting that makes inventory lifecycle changes easier for Product stakeholders to understand.

Outcome

A clearer analytical foundation for an upcoming marketplace initiative.

02

Data Analysis

Problem

Raw call logs and unstructured data were not immediately usable for reporting.

Evidence

High-volume data required cleaning, transformation, validation, and preparation for warehouse use.

Decision

Transform the raw information into structured, warehouse-ready datasets.

Outcome

More usable data for downstream reporting and analysis.

03

Validation

Problem

Reporting is only useful when stakeholders can trust the underlying data.

Evidence

I used SQL, Oracle, and VS Code to investigate discrepancies and validate outputs.

Decision

Validate the data before using it to support reporting and Product conversations.

Outcome

Greater confidence in the information presented through reporting.

04

Decision Support

Product Need

Product teams needed a more accessible way to understand inventory behavior.

Solution

Developed Power BI dashboards showing inventory lifecycle changes.

Collaboration

Worked with engineers, analysts, and Product stakeholders in an Agile/Scrum environment.

Outcome

Product stakeholders gained visibility into inventory changes that could inform marketplace planning.

Product takeaway

This project strengthened my ability to translate messy, high-volume data into information that Product teams can actually use to understand a product and make decisions.
